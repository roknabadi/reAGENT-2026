# Clean-room source policy

This hackathon workspace may use:

- Amir's general scientific and engineering knowledge
- Publicly released datasets, models, papers, and software under their terms
- Sponsor services and credits supplied for re:AGENT
- Code and analysis created specifically for the hackathon

It must not contain or derive from:

- Therna or BioReasonRNA code, datasets, weights, prompts, outputs, schemas, or credentials
- Internal URLs, buckets, screenshots, experiment results, or unpublished claims
- Reimplementations copied or mechanically translated from proprietary artifacts

Every external dataset or model used in a submission must be recorded in
`SOURCES.md` with its URL, version or commit, license/terms, retrieval date, and
checksum when practical. Public facts should be distinguishable from model
predictions and scientific inferences in both the UI and pitch.
